Frequently Asked Questions

How do I print individual exam sheets?
Go back to the My Results tab, find the particular row, and click the PDF download link.

Are certificate files included in the spreadsheet?
Spreadsheets contain metadata transcripts including scores, grades, and passing statuses, but do not contain PDF binary signatures. Certificate PDFs are separately fetched on the credentials page.
